As a result, we can apply distribtued optimization to train a base LLM. On the other hand, with a trained LLM serving for the agent, multi-agent reaonsing explore that how we can let multi-agent collaborate each other only at the inference level to effectively resolve the questions. We can see that for real-world products, we are beginning to explore the opportunities in from distributed optimization and multi-agent reasoning

### Distributed Optimization
Over the past decade, we shift from conventional training and inference of neural networks on a single machine to distributed training and inference as the scale of model and data continues to grow. We let multiple computational workers collaboratively to optimize objectives through local computation, communication, and aggregation. Today, distributed optimziation is a part of the infrastructure to train a sucessful LLM.

In general, we can have a canonical formulation where our objective is to 

$$
\min_{\theta} F(\theta)=\min_{\theta} p_i f_i(\theta)
$$

where $\theta$ is the neural network we want to distributedly optimize. We use $f_i(\theta)$ to represetn the local objective on each computation worker $i$. Then, we can have different forms of $p_i$ where they can present the important of each local objective (e.g. used for finding pareto optimal) or we can design them as $p_i=\frac{\lvert d_i \rvert}{\sum_j \lvert d_j \rvert}$ meaning the propotion of samples ($d_i$) on each devices. How $p_i$ and $f_i$ are decided depends on the actual task we want to complete.
